# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred from the brief: delegated static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, chosen for simple maintenance and fast loading. Hosting target: OpenAI Sites.

## Users

Primary users are parents and guardians of children attending the 1st Kindergarten of Kryoneri, especially on mobile devices while looking for a current announcement, school details or a way to contact the association.

Secondary users are prospective families, teachers and local community partners.

## Product Purpose

Provide one clear, friendly home for the Parents and Guardians Association of the 1st Kindergarten of Kryoneri. Success means visitors can understand the association’s role, find school and association information, read announcements and contact the right people within seconds.

## Positioning

The site is the practical digital noticeboard and gathering point for this specific kindergarten community in Kryoneri, not a general school directory.

## Operating Context

The site is read in short visits, often from a phone. Information changes by school year. Announcements, activities, documents and contact details need to be easy for non-technical association members to replace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Greek-language, responsive single-page public website.
- Sections for the school, the association, announcements and contact.
- Accessible navigation, keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
- No backend or content-management system is confirmed.
- The association’s official email, board members, constitution, social links, photos and current announcements are open decisions and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Confirmed name from the user: «Σύλλογος Γονέων & Κηδεμόνων 1ου Νηπιαγωγείου Κρυονερίου».
- The user selected 1pnc.gr as a structural and emotional reference: playful, welcoming, colourful and family-focused.
- The new work must be original and specific to Kryoneri rather than a copy of the reference site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified public school details: 1ο Νηπιαγωγείο Κρυονερίου, Αγίας Τριάδος & Υψηλάντου, 14568 Κρυονέρι, telephone 210 816 1811, school email mail@nip-kryon.att.sch.gr.
- No confirmed association logo, association email, board roster, constitution, event archive, testimonials or original photography is present in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Put urgent and current information before institutional prose.
- Make every important action obvious on a phone.
- Separate verified school facts from association-specific placeholders.
- Feel warm and child-centred without becoming visually noisy or hard to read.
- Keep content simple enough for future association volunteers to update.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, strong colour contrast, visible focus states, large touch targets, descriptive labels and full support for prefers-reduced-motion.
